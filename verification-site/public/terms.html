Prepared for publication on 5 November 2026.

Development application

Oracle is an experimental personal-assistant project operated by Sahan Pramuditha. The current application runs locally on the operator's Windows computer. This site provides project and policy information; it does not offer public hosted assistant accounts.

Account access and permitted use

Connect only accounts you are authorized to use. Google services require your consent and remain subject to Google's applicable terms, access controls, quotas and policies. Do not use Oracle to access another person's information without authorization, send abusive requests or evade service restrictions.

Review actions and results

Review destinations, dates, timezone offsets and titles before creating or changing records. AI responses, extracted text and summaries can contain errors and require checking against original sources. Oracle is not a substitute for professional advice or a safety-critical alarm system.

Availability and notifications

Features may change or be unavailable during development. Local reminders depend on the application being open. Google Calendar notifications depend on Google services and your account, application and device settings. Alert delivery, uninterrupted service and Google verification approval are not guaranteed.

Data and disconnecting

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. You may disconnect Google access through Oracle and revoke permissions in your Google Account. Disconnecting does not delete existing Google events or tasks.

Costs and support

The project is being developed using local resources and no-cost service quotas. Connecting an account does not subscribe you to a paid Oracle plan. Independently selected third-party services may have their own charges and conditions.

For questions, privacy requests or feedback, contact sahan.dev.tech@gmail.com.
