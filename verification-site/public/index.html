Personal assistant · In development

Calendar and tasks,
connected from your computer.

Oracle brings conversation, notes, reminders and image reading into a local workspace. Connect your Google account when you want to work with Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

The assistant currently runs on the project owner's Windows computer. This site explains the application and its data practices; it is not a hosted copy of the assistant or a public sign-up service.

Work locally

Create and retrieve notes, manage local timers and reminders, read text from images, and save image memories explicitly. Local records use the application's own storage.

Connect deliberately

Google sign-in identifies your account and requests Calendar and Tasks permissions together. Google access is optional. You choose when to load records and when to create or change them.

Google account permissions

What the Google connection does

AccessPurpose in Oracle
Account identityConfirm your Google account and display its verified email address.
Calendar list and eventsChoose a calendar, display upcoming events, and create events with a notification offset you select.
Tasks and task listsChoose a task list, display and create tasks, and mark a task complete or reopen it.

A local reminder can prefill a Google Calendar event for your review. Creating the event is a separate action. Google and local records do not automatically synchronize.

Timed reminders use Calendar. Google Tasks due dates are date-only through the Tasks API. Calendar notifications depend on your Google apps, account settings and devices; Oracle cannot guarantee alert delivery.

Google data accessed by this connector is not sent to Oracle's AI models, used to train models, sold, or used for advertising. Read the privacy policy for access, storage, retention and deletion details.

Availability and support

Oracle is a development project. Google sign-in is initiated inside the local application under Workspace → Google. Availability of Google account access depends on Google's consent, quota and verification requirements.
